Defaults

Basic is the normal card type It creates one card: front → back. Reversed and cloze must be selected.

All 5 formats are on Atomic, inline, fenced, hashtag, and cloze work without setup.

Values used when you do not change settings

01 / Atomic

Title card

Uses the file name as the question. Uses the first paragraph as the answer.

Basic 1 card 
---
test:
  - title
---

Chlorophyll absorbs light energy to drive the reactions.
Front · Photosynthesis.md

Photosynthesis

Chlorophyll absorbs light energy to drive the reactions.

04 / Atomic

Whole-note cloze

Makes a cloze from the first paragraph. Shows the file name with the answer.

Cloze 1 card Explicit 
---
test: cloze
---

Chlorophyll is a ==pigment== that absorbs light.
Front · cloze 1

Chlorophyll is a […] that absorbs light.

Chlorophyll is a pigment that absorbs light.

06 / Classic

Fenced block

Use this format for long text. type: is optional. If it is missing, the card is basic.

Basic 1 card type: omitted 
```flashcard
front: What is the CAP theorem?
back: A distributed store provides at most two of
  Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance.
```
Basic · type defaults to basic

What is the CAP theorem?

A distributed store provides at most two of Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance.

08 / Classic

Cloze sentence

Hide text with ==text== or {number:text}. Spans with the same number are hidden on the same card.

Cloze 2 cards Explicit marks 
The mitochondria is the ==powerhouse== of the cell.

The {1:powerhouse} of the cell is the {1:mitochondria}.
Auto cloze · card 1 of 2

The mitochondria is the […] of the cell.

The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell.

Numbered cloze · card 2 of 2

The […] of the cell is the […].

The powerhouse of the cell is the mitochondria.
